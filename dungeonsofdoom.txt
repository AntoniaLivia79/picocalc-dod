Dungeon of Doom

Ported from BBC BASIC to PicoMite BASIC

DoD consists of 3 BASIC programs. By default
all 3 should be copied to a new folder on
the root of the SD Card called DOOM (the
path can be changed as shown below). The
first time you run the game, you will
need to run the programs in the order
shown below to generate the level and
character data files. After that,
you can just run DOOM.BAS to play the game. 

DUNGEN.BAS
----------

The level designer — writes LEVEL1.DAT 
to LEVELn.DAT 

CHARGEN.BAS
-----------

The character generator — rolls and 
equips a hero — writes HERO.DAT

DOOM.BAS
--------

The game module loads both the LEVEL and
HERO file, and saves progress.

Every path lives in two variables at the
top of each program:

Dim DPATH$ = "B:/DOOM"
Dim SPATH$ = "B:/DOOM/SAVE"

On PicoMite A: is internal flash and B:
is the SD card. 

Using the Level designer
----------------------

The cursor is driven with the arrows or
H J K L and the digit keys lay tiles:

1 WALL 
2 VASE
3 TREASURE
4 LOST IDOL
5 ENTRANCE
6 EXIT
7 TRAP
8 SAFE PLACE
9 MONSTER
0 ERASE a tile

E wipes the level, N saves and goes one
level deeper, S saves and stops, ? 
shows the lore.

Levels already saved are loaded for
editing instead of always starting 
from blank rock, so you can go back 
and revise a level without redrawing it.

Q quits without saving the level in
progress.

A level cannot be saved until a way
in has been placed. The LOST IDOL ( 4 )
ends the quest, so it belongs on your
deepest level.

DUNGEON.DAT is written alongside the
levels and just records how many
there are, so the game can show 
"LEVEL 2 OF 5".

Saving and resuming
------------------

Press S in the game. It writes the 
current map and the hero into 
B:/DOOM/SAVE/ , then stops. 

On the next run, DOOM.BAS offers
Resume or New.

The generated levels in B:/DOOM/ are 
never overwritten — the game tries to 
read a level from SAVE if one is there and
falls back to the original level file
otherwise, so starting a new hero gives
you a fresh dungeon without re-running
DUNGEN.BAS.

Rolling a new character, or redrawing a
level, clears the matching save.

Playing the game
----------------

The game is played in real time — monsters
move every tick whether you act or not.
Tarry not.

Screen layout
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The dungeon occupies the left 15x15 grid.
A status panel runs down the right side:

  STR   current hit points
  VIT   vitality (max hit points, depletes
        slowly when struck)
  AURA  magical energy (needed for spells)
  FACE  direction you are facing: N E S W
  EXP   experience
  GOLD  gold carried
  TRSR  treasure pieces found

The bottom bar shows:

  ATT   total attack power (weapon + STR)
  SPL   spell charges remaining
  LGT   lamp oil charges
  POT   potions and salves

Your hero (@) changes sprite to show
which way you face.

Movement
~~~~~~~~

You move one square at a time in the
direction you are currently facing.

  UP ARROW or M   move forward
  LEFT ARROW or B turn left (widdershins)
  RIGHT ARROW or N turn right (sunwise)

You cannot walk into walls, monsters, or
loose objects — bumping them costs a
sliver of STR.

What you see
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Only squares you have already lit are
shown. Unexplored passages stay dark.
Tiles you may encounter:

  @   you
  #   wall (impassable)
  !   flask — contains a salve and potion
  $   treasure
  &   the Lost Idol — your quest goal
  *   entrance (the way in)
  >   stairs down to the next level
  ^   trap
  O   magic circle — safe from monster
      attacks while you stand on it
  a b c  monsters (weakest to worst)

Walking onto the stair tile (>) descends
to the next level automatically.

A trap (^) snares you in place, draining
STR, until a lucky Agility check frees you.

Combat
~~~~~~

  A   attack the monster one square ahead

Attack connects if your Agility plus Luck
beats the monster's roll; a miss does no
damage. A successful hit does damage equal
to your STR plus weapons, minus the
monster's toughness. Monsters automatically
strike back when adjacent — armour and a
helmet reduce what gets through.

A monster can smash one piece of your
equipment when it hits.

When a monster dies its EXP bonus is added
to your total.

Picking things up
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

  G or DOWN ARROW   grab what is one
                    square ahead of you

  Flask (!)   adds one salve and one
              potion to your stores
  Treasure ($)  adds one piece of treasure
  Lost Idol (&) ends the quest in victory

Potions and salves
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

  P   quaff — uses a potion if STR is
      below maximum, otherwise uses a
      salve if VIT is below maximum

Potions restore STR to its peak.
Salves restore VIT to its peak.

Lamp and darkness
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

  R   reveal — lights the 7x7 area around
      you, costs one charge of lamp oil

Without lamp oil R shows "NO LIGHT".
Squares you have already visited stay
visible without spending oil.

Spells
~~~~~~

  C   cast a spell

You must have AURA above zero and carry
at least one spellbook or scroll set.
The Necronomicon gives spells 1-3; the
Scrolls give spells 4-6.

  1 SMITE      instantly slays the monster
               in front of you
  2 WARD       lays a magic circle (O)
               beneath your feet
  3 TRANSPORT  whisks you to a random
               square on the level
  4 MEND       heals a small amount of
               STR and VIT
  5 CHAOS      randomly remakes the tile
               directly ahead — can
               clear a monster or an
               obstacle
  6 RENEWAL    fully restores STR and VIT

Each spell costs one charge; casting with
no charges left wastes the action.
Spells 4 and 6 also drain one point of
AURA.

Key reference (in-game ? shows this)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

  UP / M    move forward
  LEFT / B  turn left
  RIGHT / N turn right
  DOWN / G  pick up ahead
  A         attack
  C         cast spell
  P         quaff potion or salve
  R         reveal area (costs lamp oil)
  S         save and stop
  ?         show this lore screen

Victory and defeat
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Grab the Lost Idol (&) on the deepest
level to complete the quest. Your final
score is:

  (treasure x 10) + (gold x experience)
  + remaining STR + VIT + Agility

If STR reaches zero your hero expires.
The level and depth at which they fell
are recorded on screen.
